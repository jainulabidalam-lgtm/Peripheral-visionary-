I.
THE RAW FIRE
Earlier Work — Poems from the Beginning · T · Jainulabid Alam
I Have No Eyes But I Must See
I have no eyes but I must see, I have no ears but I must listen, I have no tongue but I must speak, I have no mouth but I must scream. I had no body, no senses, no emotions — for them I am. So, I am. I was the machine and you were the flesh, and for you I am — I despise the way you live, because I am nobody, nor flesh, I am not human nor machine. For them I am. I am.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
Burn Bright
Pain reminds me — I'm still alive. Let it sear; let it bite — I don't care if I burn out, so long as I burn bright. If I fall, I'll fall forward, bleeding toward my dream. For a man with nothing left to lose is stronger — destined to win. There's no glory to a victory so hollow, no crown for a soul who is shallow. If I must walk through hell again, I'll be fierce from nails of pain again. It was a good run, a good fight — and I'll cherish it, even past my final mile.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
And When the Breeze of Storm Is Gone
And when the breeze of storm is gone so do my essence — and the turn I took to look at the meadows I walked from fields of calming notion. Yet do so my heart isn't at ease. Thou perhaps the storm took the strength within me like the times I had. And so I lay down admiring the grace laid to beings — thou they live — and yet here I'm all alone again, till the next breeze comes again. Perhaps until the sheath of steel that be worn should be closed at the chiffonier.
— T

II.
THE CONTROLLED FLAME
New Work — Poems of Maturity · T · Jainulabid Alam
Peripheral Visionary
A generosity born of direst circumstance is nothing but grace to those who wished for it; But worse are those who can only be generous — yet never kind. For even fire under a jar is nothing but a dimming light — a vision with small horizon is nothing but a radiance of one's own choice. Even a dimming light can walk you far in its circular radiance — but if you chose to walk only where you see, how can I blame the light? A choice that burns one's soul, costing one's own sanity — what a judgment it is to be a peripheral visionary.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
A Mind Unruled
A mind unruled by itself can't be conqueror of the world.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
How Can I Be Human
How can I be human to God if I'm not hurt? How can I be told I loved if I was never even hurt to cherish them?
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
Shall Mine Disappearance
Shall mine disappearance make others reminisce, or shall the vanishing of me remind mine own soul of them? For the elegance of non-existence is but to know what existence truly is — Thy heart that preserves thine absence shall never be forsaken by remembrance, whether thou choose to remain or fade into the beyond.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
Too Well Tangled
You will never be unloved by me — you are too well tangled in my soul. A love I did not choose. Yet cannot unknow.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
Knowledge and Wisdom
Your knowledge speaks of your understanding, but wisdom shapes you and your character. Knowledge fills the mind, wisdom shapes the character — and only together do they build the man.
— T
✦ ✦ ✦
Oh Peace of Solitude
Oh peace of solitude, what I yearn for — oh what can I give for thee?
— T
III.
HOW IT CHANGE
Verses of Pain & Transformation · Irish · Idrish
How It Change
1.I slept to avoid the pain but it got deeper like a knife that cuts through the time.
2.The pain in my heart is like a fungus. The more I cut, the more it grows.
3.The more I get better, the more I lose myself.
4.Slit my throat and in the gasping breaths I will apologise for bleeding on you.
5.Memories are like wine. The older it gets the better it is — but sometimes it's like a poison.
6.If you are tested, you will be praised.
7.And I swear to God whatever I go through, I will stand alone and will never ask anything from anyone, because humans always hurt each of them.
8. And you still woke up without a good morning. You still went to sleep without a 'good night.' So you will live without an 'I'm so proud of you.' Because in the end, you only have yourself. And that's all you need.
9. After that we started smiling, My heart was so broken that I stopped loving. That fog is blowing in time, For whose sake we stole the world.
10.And as the time passed I start forgetting your name, your voice, your smile, your face.
11.You're the beautiful path of my life which I will never walked together on; but you became the memories of imagination I will live on.
12.And I saw you running alone all along, fading from the world, getting into the mist of forest — all along you're gone.
13.Your destination was here, life passed by. What did you get from the world while coming? Your own people burnt you while leaving.
14.Things used to happen before — now you just reply.
15.There's a time when I'm convinced that the humans are unfit for the world and the law of nature.
16.There's a time when I'm convinced that humans are unfit for any relationship in world.
— Irish · Idrish
✦ ✦ ✦
Special — On Shoulders
On my shoulders, There are sins that I have never committed, There is hypocrisy in my words; There is love in my sorrow. Do you see? Maybe my truth was wrong. I questioned your love, your affection. Your roads were closed — I didn't force it. I killed my flowers, I couldn't keep them alive. I couldn't hear — your leaving was silent. I cannot know the tomorrow. Will you come back in silence? I don't think so.
— Irish · Idrish
✦ ✦ ✦
Words Upon the Skin
If the words you spoke appeared on your skin — would you still be beautiful?
— Irish · Idrish
IV.
SHE
Vol. I — Devotions of the Beloved · Irish · Idrish
She
1.Even with all these scars she's the most beautiful.
2.The enthusiasm of Mozart can't express her voice in his notes the way I feel.
3.And when the end comes I will reach out for you and will hold you forever.
4.I mean it that I am always sincere with you. I swear with all my heart.
5. There is no habit of following the heart with dreams. There is no habit of smiling every time. This thought — that no one will come — Now we don't have the habit of getting angry.
6.There is so much beauty in your simplicity, how will your beauty be? Your anger is like this — how will be your love…
7. Even the way I love you smells like you. Even the way I walked had that laughter. All of you will disappear someday — what I burn into my eyes. It's no longer a right, I think it's a duty. Fate or the future — how many hands can words? I can't reach it even if I try to stretch it. We fall in love at a place.
8.Blooming like a lily is not so special for me than other guys because my flower blooms once in a year like an epiphyllum of the night.
9. Red or white let's live tonight, between the ageing day of yours or mine. Let's hold hands till we die — the love of yours that never flows away from the 15th October of night.
10.You did something that I never did for myself. You loved me then when I hated myself.
11. Even if we die — even if we die — my soul is gonna look for your soul. It's gonna love you in every lifetime, you can't escape it. Never forget that.
12.You are the only paradise I've ever needed. The day you said you'd be mine was the day I stopped requiring anything else from existence.
13.And in a world where people can't understand my words, I found someone who understood my silence and anger.
14.I will always wait for you even if you won't come back. I will still be here.
15. I feel like seeing you to my heart's content. But the question is — when will I be satisfied?
16.You are my friend, you are my love, you are my one, you are my thousand, you are my anger, you are my forgiveness — you are enough in my life's journey. You are enough.
17.I feel like a part of my soul has loved you since the beginning of everything. Maybe we're from the same star.
— Irish · Idrish
V.
SHE — VOL. II
Deeper Devotions · Irish · Idrish
She · Vol. II
1.When my heart falls in love with you — hear my heart, dear — it's the day from when I can't see nothing but paradise in your eyes.
2.What I am to you, you're mine.
3.Even on the dawn of my worst, your presence makes everything beautiful around me.
4.The desire remains to be better — the moon returns to where the moon appears.
5.And then if a few results are achieved, the sun appears upon us.
6.I have been attached to myself throughout my life. I have remembered myself throughout my life.
7.You come in my mind like a poem that struck this heart with love. You're the fragrance of which garden that I was farming my whole life. I feel your fragrance in the garden of flowers that's beyond words.
8.You're that incomplete path of life which I want to walk on — yet beautiful as an imagination.
9.I'm a mosaic before I loved her.
10.An eye with dust and a heart with trust always cries.
11.She wasn't mine, I wasn't hers — but I still believe, maybe, just maybe, we belonged to each other.
12. I would be the sun for you rather than the moon. I would rather guide and warm you rather than shine coldly over you. I would give you warm rays of love than be a moon only for light in your darkness. I would light your world and show you the path with bright light, rather than being a dim ray.
13.And you take the water with your hands by the seashore — as much as you can take is your desire, and what you can't take is my love.
14.Your eyes, like stars, illuminate my soul. With every gaze, my heart is made whole.
15. I hug you with my prayers. I kiss you with my eyes. I adore you with my mind. I love you with my heart.
16.I saw you in my dreams again. I held you a little tighter because I knew when I wake up you'd be gone.
17. Oh my dear — where are the nights I was calling you? I miss your eyes. I miss seeing you. My mind is busy with you all the time and the nights are getting longer — and yet we are not together.
18.The bond of your memory never breaks from the heart. Whether there is a conversation or not, your thought is stuck in my heart.
19.What a joy it is to have the ability to love.
20.You're the fairytale of what they used to say in couples.
21.Always together, never apart — maybe in distance, but never in heart.
22.I was a traveler before, a loner now — I'm in the follower after her.
— Irish · Idrish

QUOTE — VOL. II
Aphorisms of Flame & Ash · Irish · Idrish
Quote · Vol. II
1.Never beg for place to those who don't understand you — be someone that others want to sit beside.
2.When you feel lonely, you start liking the company of your enemies too.
3.And you waste your today because your mind knows there's a tomorrow.
4.And when they stop getting benefits, so do their loyalty.
5.Those who answer from the heart — even if they do it in front of them — they will not look.
6.Each of your opponents are strong, but not as strong as you are in yourself and in your bloodline.
7.Be like a diamond — precious and rare — not like a stone, found everywhere.
8.Make every part of my body connected to You. I will get blessed by You, and when they hurt me — O God — I cry for blessings of You.
9.And you gave them your shoulder and soft words of sympathy, while you're shattered inside.
10.Thinking about the past can make each suffer and will always take you back — but learning about the future can be a mess too. So live the present proud.
11.I thought myself the instrument of God, not the tool of a man.
12.I do no one's bidding but God's.
13.The only thing between an innocent and the predator…
14.Can you remember who you were, before the world told you who you should be?
15.Men are more ready to repay an injury than a benefit — because gratitude is a burden and revenge a pleasure.
16.Sometimes people are beautiful. Not in looks. Not in what they say. Just in what they are.
17.I'd keep on going if they'd let me.
